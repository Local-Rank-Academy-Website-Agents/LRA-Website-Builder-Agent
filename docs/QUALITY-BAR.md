# Website Quality Bar

Use this as the acceptance checklist for every delivered site.

## Visual quality

- The design matches the niche, market, and intended customer.
- Typography, spacing, color, imagery, icons, and components form a consistent system.
- The home page has a clear visual hierarchy and no filler sections.
- Mobile and desktop layouts are intentionally composed, not merely resized.
- Headers, menus, sticky elements, modals, and forms do not overlap or clip.
- Empty, loading, success, validation, and error states look finished.

## Conversion

- The primary offer, service area, proof, and CTA are visible without hunting.
- Phone, form, and other lead actions work on relevant devices.
- CTA labels describe the next step.
- Forms ask only for information needed to qualify and respond.
- Trust claims are specific, verifiable, and placed near decision points.

## Content and local SEO

- Every indexable page has a distinct purpose and useful original content.
- Titles, descriptions, H1s, canonicals, social metadata, and URLs are intentional.
- Services and locations are linked through a logical information architecture.
- FAQs answer real purchase or service questions and are not keyword padding.
- Structured data matches visible, verified page content.
- Sitemap and robots directives match the intended indexation strategy.
- No fake address, fake review, unsupported credential, or guaranteed result appears.

## Performance and accessibility

- Images use suitable formats, dimensions, compression, and lazy loading where appropriate.
- Fonts are limited, optimized, and do not cause avoidable layout shift.
- Interactive elements work with keyboard input and show focus.
- Controls have accessible names; images have appropriate alternative text.
- Text and UI colors meet practical contrast standards.
- Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
- The production build has no avoidable console errors.

## Engineering

- A clean install and production build succeed using documented commands.
- Dependencies and lockfiles are committed.
- No credentials, tokens, private keys, or machine-specific absolute paths are included.
- The output directory can be deployed independently when the stack supports it.
- Redirects, rewrites, headers, form handlers, and environment variables are documented.
- A useful 404 page and favicon/application icons are included.

## Handoff

- Source and production output are both present.
- `README` includes local development, build, and deployment instructions.
- The handoff identifies launch-time values still required.
- Forms, analytics, consent, domain, DNS, and ownership transfer are documented.
- A post-deployment smoke-test list is included.
- Known limitations are stated explicitly.

# Build Request

Build a polished, portable static lead-generation website for **Houston Mold Remediation Pros**.

## Conversion journey

1. Confirm the broad mold-remediation topic and Greater Houston market immediately.
2. Help visitors choose a distinct service based on property type, affected material, building system, moisture source, or decision.
3. Explain how assessment, remediation, source repair, drying, HVAC work, and verification may involve different roles.
4. Link each service to its core related services and all applicable regional service-area destinations.
5. Invite the visitor to organize a request in the non-routing test form.

## Sitemap

- `/` — Broad Greater Houston service intent and conversion overview
- `/services/` — Complete service directory
- 18 keyword-mapped service routes recorded in `KEYWORD-MAP.md`
- `/houston-service-area/` — Seven differentiated regions with 33 intended community anchors
- `/process/` — Transparent matching and verification workflow
- `/contact/` — Form-first lead action
- `/privacy/` — Pre-launch data-handling disclosure
- `/404.html` — Useful not-found route

## Content architecture

- Homepage H1: `Mold Remediation Services in the Greater Houston Area`
- Every service H1 begins with its mapped service phrase and uses Houston naturally.
- Semantic H2/H3 headings cover property conditions, materials, moisture pathways, scope decisions, and process concepts without targeting another page's primary keyword.
- Every substantive page has a visible, unique FAQ section near the bottom.
- Every service page contains visible Related Services and Service Areas sections.
- Desktop navigation, accessible mobile navigation, and the footer contain complete crawlable service and area directories generated from one maintained data source.
- Area candidates remain one regional guide with meaningful sections until an operator verifies coverage and separate location pages can be genuinely differentiated.

## Visual direction

Use a calm editorial art direction with deep evergreen, mineral green, warm ivory, amber accents, large typography, restrained rounded controls, generous whitespace, and an original hero photograph. The reference site informs freshness, CTA clarity, service navigation, and section rhythm only.

## Build constraints

- Awaiting operator: do not imply that an active local contractor exists.
- Do not publish a phone, street address, review, license, certification, guarantee, price, staff biography, project history, emergency claim, or GBP claim without verification.
- Keep the site `noindex` and disallow crawling until launch blockers are resolved.
- Keep the form non-routing until a verified destination is configured.
- Treat all 33 communities as intended targets requiring operator confirmation, not active coverage claims.
- Use truthful `Organization`, `WebSite`, `Service`, `CollectionPage`, and `BreadcrumbList` structured data only.
- Deployment is not in scope.

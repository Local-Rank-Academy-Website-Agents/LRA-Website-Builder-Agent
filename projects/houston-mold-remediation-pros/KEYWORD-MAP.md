## Cannibalization decisions

- `mold removal Houston` is merged into `/services/mold-remediation/`; no competing removal page is created.
- Residential, commercial, and apartment pages remain separate because property type, stakeholders, access, documentation, and conversion needs differ.
- HVAC mold remediation and air-duct cleaning remain separate because contaminated mechanical materials and routine source-removal cleaning require different scopes.
- Mold inspection/testing owns Texas assessment and protocol intent; indoor-air-quality testing owns broader environmental measurement intent.
- Post-remediation verification is limited to independent completion review and does not target initial inspection intent.
- Mold odor removal owns source-tracing and odor-treatment intent; it does not claim that every musty odor is mold.
- Crawl-space remediation owns contaminated-material cleanup; encapsulation owns the moisture-control assembly installed after diagnosis and correction.
- The service-area architecture uses one differentiated regional guide with anchors. Separate city pages are deferred until an operator verifies coverage and genuinely distinct local content is available.

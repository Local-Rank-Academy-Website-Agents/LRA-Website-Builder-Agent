Privacy Information for Toilet Repair Houston Pros

This page explains the current HubSpot form routing and the data boundaries for this independent service-request website.

Information collected through forms

The live request form may collect a name, email address or phone number, property location, selected service, and a description of the situation. After browser-side validation, those fields and the source page are sent to HubSpot for lead intake and follow-up.

Purpose and potential provider sharing

Submitted information is used to understand the request, check service and market fit, respond to the person who contacted the website, and maintain lead records. It may be available to the project owner, HubSpot, and a provider selected to review or fulfill the request.

Sensitive information

Do not submit payment-card details, bank information, passwords, alarm or gate codes, government identifiers, medical information, or other sensitive data. Share only the contact and property details reasonably needed to evaluate the request.

Cookies, analytics, and calls

No general analytics, remarketing, advertising, or session-recording scripts are included. A controlled phone line is not published in this build. If call tracking or recording is added, the visible disclosure and this page must be updated before activation.

Retention, security, and requests

Lead records are retained within the connected services according to the project owner's operational settings and applicable obligations. Use the contact form to request access, correction, or deletion and provide enough information to identify the original submission.

Frequently asked questions

Privacy questions

Current form collection, HubSpot routing, sensitive information, tracking, and data requests.

What does the form collect?+

It may collect a name, contact method, property location, selected service, and situation details needed for lead intake and follow-up.

Where is a valid form sent?+

The form sends submitted fields and the source page to HubSpot for lead intake. The browser does not intentionally store the request in local storage or cookies.

Are calls recorded?+

No phone number is published until a controlled tracking line is assigned. If recording is enabled later, the privacy notice and caller disclosure must be updated first.

Are analytics or advertising trackers installed?+

No general analytics, remarketing, advertising, or session-recording scripts are included in this static build.

How can a data request be made?+

Use the website form and identify the submission you want accessed, corrected, or deleted. Do not include new sensitive information in the request.

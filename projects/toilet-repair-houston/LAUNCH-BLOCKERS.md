# Launch Blockers

- Purchase and confirm the final domain.
- Verify DNS and add the custom domain to Vercel.
- Change `indexingEnabled` to `true`, set the preferred www/apex canonical, rebuild, and redeploy.
- Verify the production domain in Google Search Console and submit `/sitemap.xml`.
- Assign a Local Rank Academy-controlled phone number before publishing a call CTA.
- Verify the operator, exact coverage, licensing, insurance, hours, permits, and service claims before adding them.

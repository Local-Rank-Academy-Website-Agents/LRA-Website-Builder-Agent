Pre-launch data handling

Privacy Information for This Test Build

This static test build does not transmit form entries and has no analytics, advertising pixels, call tracking, CRM, or contractor recipient.

Current browser-only behavior

The contact form validates required fields in the browser and displays a local status message. It does not send values to a server, email service, webhook, customer relationship management system, or operator.

Information planned for a live request

A future form may collect a name, contact method, property ZIP code, property type, service selection, and a short description. The production owner must identify the controller, processors, recipients, purpose, retention period, and deletion route before collection begins.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

No measurement or advertising tools are installed. Consent, opt-out, and disclosure requirements must be reviewed before analytics, call tracking, remarketing, session recording, or conversion pixels are added.

Security and minimization

The production system should collect only what is needed to respond, protect transmissions, limit recipient access, filter spam, avoid sensitive details, and establish a documented deletion schedule.

Required privacy contact

No active public email, phone, or customer-facing address has been verified. A working privacy contact and effective date are launch blockers.

Frequently asked questions

Privacy questions for Plumber Houston Pros

Plain-language limits of the current test build.

Are test entries stored in the browser?+

The script reads field values only to validate them and show a status message. It does not intentionally place the entry in cookies or local storage.

Does the site share leads with plumbers?+

No. There is no connected provider or lead destination.

Will a live version use cookies?+

That decision has not been made. The privacy notice and consent controls must be updated before any nonessential tracking is introduced.

Where can I submit a privacy request?+

There is no active contact yet. The project owner must add and verify one before live data collection.

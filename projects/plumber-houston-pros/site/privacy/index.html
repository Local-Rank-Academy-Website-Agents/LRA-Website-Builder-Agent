Call and form data handling

Privacy Information for Plumber Houston Pros

This independent service-request website uses a tracked forwarding number and a HubSpot form endpoint to receive and follow up on plumbing requests.

Information collected through the form

The live request form may collect your name, email address or phone number, property ZIP code, property type, selected service, and a short description. After browser-side validation, those fields and the page URL are sent to HubSpot for lead intake, notification, and follow-up.

Calls and call routing

The phone number displayed on this website is a CallRail tracking number that forwards calls for intake. Calls may be recorded for quality and lead-handling purposes; when recording is enabled, callers receive a recording disclosure. Call metadata and recordings are processed within the connected CallRail account.

Purpose and sharing

Submitted information is used to understand the request, confirm service-area and provider availability, respond to the person who contacted the site, and maintain lead records. Information may be available to the project owner, HubSpot, CallRail, and a provider selected to review or fulfill the request. Do not include payment information, access codes, medical details, or other sensitive data.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

No general analytics, remarketing, session-recording, or advertising pixels are included in this static build. HubSpot receives form data only when the form is submitted, and CallRail processes calls placed to the tracked number.

Retention, security, and requests

Lead and call records are retained within the connected services according to the project owner's operational settings and applicable obligations. Use the website contact form or tracked phone number to ask about access, correction, or deletion of a submitted request.

Frequently asked questions

Privacy questions for Plumber Houston Pros

Plain-language details about the live call and form intake.

Are form entries stored in the browser?+

The script reads field values for validation but does not intentionally save the request in cookies or local storage. A valid submission is sent to HubSpot.

Does the site share request details with a provider?+

Request details may be shared with a provider selected to review or fulfill the request. Availability and qualifications must be confirmed before scheduling.

Are calls recorded?+

The tracked phone line may record calls for quality and lead handling. When recording is enabled, callers receive a recording disclosure.

Where can I submit a privacy request?+

Use the contact form or tracked phone number and identify the request you want accessed, corrected, or deleted.

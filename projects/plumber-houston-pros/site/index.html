Frequently asked questions

Greater Houston plumbing questions

Broad planning answers before you select a service or market page.

Is Plumber Houston Pros the plumbing contractor?+

No. It is an independent plumbing information and service-request website, not the contractor that performs the work. Calls and forms are routed for follow-up, and availability is confirmed before scheduling.

What counts as a plumbing emergency?+

Active flooding, suspected gas, sewage exposure, or loss of essential service may require urgent action. Use safe shutoffs when you know how, avoid hazards, and contact appropriate emergency or utility resources.

Why are there separate drain and sewer pages?+

A fixture branch stoppage, main-line restriction, camera inspection, jetting job, and structural sewer repair have different intent and scope.

Which Houston areas are mapped?+

The directory covers 43 markets across the Houston core and north, west, southwest, south, southeast, and east regions. Exact service availability is confirmed from the ZIP code and requested work.

Can I call about a plumbing request?+

Yes. Use the call button or contact form. The request is routed for follow-up; submitting it does not guarantee immediate dispatch or that every service is available in every ZIP code.

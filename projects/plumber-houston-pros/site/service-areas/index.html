Frequently asked questions

Greater Houston plumbing coverage questions

How intended service areas are organized and activated responsibly.

Does a page mean a plumber is already available there?+

No. Every market is an intended target awaiting an operator whose actual ZIP-code coverage and services must be confirmed.

Why use city and neighborhood pages?+

They resolve different local intent. Incorporated cities can have their own permitting and utility context, while large Houston districts have distinct property and access conditions.

How are overlapping place names handled?+

Each approved place owns one canonical page. Broad mailing names such as Spring or Katy explicitly require a ZIP code, subdivision, and jurisdiction for accurate routing.

Will every service be offered in every market?+

That is the planned architecture, but publication as active coverage must wait until the selected operator confirms each service-market combination.

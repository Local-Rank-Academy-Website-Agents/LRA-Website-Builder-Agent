Frequently asked questions

Hydro Jetting questions for Greater Houston

Answers to common service, scope, and property questions.

How do I choose the right hydro jetting service?+

Start with the observable symptom, equipment or pipe involved, property type, and desired outcome. Each service guide explains where that option fits and what should be documented.

What information should I gather before requesting service?+

Record when the issue began, which systems are affected, any visible leakage or damage, previous work, equipment labels, access limitations, and the property ZIP code.

Which Greater Houston communities are included?+

The service-area directory covers 43 Houston districts, incorporated cities, suburbs, and nearby communities, each with distinct property and access context.

What should a written service scope include?+

Look for clear testing, access, materials, permit responsibilities, shutdowns, cleanup, completion evidence, and a process for concealed conditions.
